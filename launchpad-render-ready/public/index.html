Practical strategy for each round

A repeatable approach for your next interview

Online assessmentRead constraints first, budget time by question, verify edge cases, and reserve minutes to test. Follow the current invitation?s rules.
Coding and DSAClarify inputs, explain a straightforward approach, improve it, state complexity, then dry-run normal and boundary cases.
Technical and system designStart from fundamentals. For design, clarify users and scale, sketch components, discuss data, trade-offs, failure handling, and monitoring.
Projects and r?sum?Be precise about your own contribution. Explain the problem, decisions, alternatives, measurable result, and what you would change.
Behavioral and HRUse STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep actions specific, be honest about lessons, and connect motivation to the role.
Group discussionMake a concise opening point, support it with an example, listen actively, invite others, and help the group reach a synthesis.
